## Cause and change

Verified in installed Iris bytecode and live captures: `MixinModelViewBobbing` calls
`camera.viewRotationMatrix.mulLocal(bobStack)` just before invoking `LevelRenderer.render`.
Reflexium's projection-upload capture runs earlier. During the pack walks, drawn projection
effects are false and drawn view effects are true. History previously omitted that late bob.
The existing `LevelRendererOutlineMixin` now records the passed view at render HEAD and rebuilds
both view-projections from the already recorded projections. No synthetic bob, extra jitter,
camera-position offset, hand-path change or new dependency is involved.

Consumer audit: camera and cloud reprojection, ObjectMotion's raster/depth gate and current/previous
clips, `foliageProjection` uniforms, Streamline SR/FG and legacy FG clip-to-previous constants,
depth resolve and capture all use this shared history after the level draw. DH's `dhToMain`
uses corrected current VP times inverse DH VP, while its own previous DH motion remains independent.
DH was absent live. BSL foliage was inspected but not live-tested in this pass.
DeferredWorldText, DeferredEffects and VanillaCloudMotion already read the late camera state.
Cut detection still uses physical position/orientation; ordinary bob must not create cuts.

Orientation constants remain physical. Bundled `native/streamline/ProgrammingGuideDLSS_G.md`
section 20 documents their orthonormal basis as input to automatic scene-change detection.
The corrected clip reprojection carries bob motion. There is no measured reason to perturb
the physical basis, especially for non-rotational nausea scaling. No generated-frame result
is claimed from that decision.

1.12.0 + Sodium 0.9.2 + Meshelium 1.6.2, Complementary r5.9.3 + Euphoria 1.10.5.

no overlay. UP input 640x360; Quality 1280x720. Reflex interval 16667 us, about 60 real FPS.
This is not Ryan's 2560x1440 output or an FPS benchmark.

Each row teleports to `200.5 151 1.5`, yaw 0, pitch 3, settles 1500 ms, presses forward,
and makes five input/output/vector/depth captures, with 120 ms waits after each readback.
First capture is stationary; the table uses the four moving captures. Readback duration varies,
so these are the same start pose and command sequence, not frame-exact bob phases or end poses.

Score: mean of per-frame median vector error, in render pixels, over the central static range
crop (middle half horizontally, middle third vertically; zero depth skipped). `drawn-view-check`
reconstructs from the actual drawn view, restores physical camera translation and projects through
the previous actual drawn view, independently of the possibly wrong history used for motion.
Encoded vectors have 1/32-pixel steps. This measures vector correctness, not image warp residual.
