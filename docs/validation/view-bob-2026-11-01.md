## Cause and change

Verified in installed Iris bytecode and live captures: `MixinModelViewBobbing` calls
`camera.viewRotationMatrix.mulLocal(bobStack)` just before invoking `LevelRenderer.render`.
Reflexium's projection-upload capture runs earlier. During the pack walks, drawn projection
effects are false and drawn view effects are true. History previously omitted that late bob.
The existing `LevelRendererOutlineMixin` now records the passed view at render HEAD and rebuilds
both view-projections from the already recorded projections. No synthetic bob, extra jitter,
camera-position offset, hand-path change or new dependency is involved.

Consumer audit: camera and cloud reprojection, ObjectMotion's raster/depth gate and current/previous
clips, `foliageProjection` uniforms, Streamline SR/FG and legacy FG clip-to-previous constants,
depth resolve and capture all use this shared history after the level draw. DH's `dhToMain`
uses corrected current VP times inverse DH VP, while its own previous DH motion remains independent.
DH was absent live. BSL foliage was inspected but not live-tested in this pass.
DeferredWorldText, DeferredEffects and VanillaCloudMotion already read the late camera state.
Cut detection still uses physical position/orientation; ordinary bob must not create cuts.

Orientation constants remain physical. Bundled `native/streamline/ProgrammingGuideDLSS_G.md`
section 20 documents their orthonormal basis as input to automatic scene-change detection.
The corrected clip reprojection carries bob motion. There is no measured reason to perturb
the physical basis, especially for non-rotational nausea scaling. No generated-frame result
is claimed from that decision.

### Natural hillside shimmer

The specified pose `(200.5,110,150)`, yaw90/pitch10 does contain natural hills; `followup/hill-pose.png`
shows them. An invisible barrier walkway in this isolated save supports survival walking at y110,
so the player produces ordinary bob without falling or changing the distant scene. FG is off here.
Both arms use six stationary captures and six walking captures from the reset pose, 100 ms waits
after readback. The initial stationary walking capture is excluded, leaving five moving frames.
Readback duration and bob phases are not exactly matched across sessions.

Preselected exposed grass/dirt ROI `(1030,760,70,120)` avoids foliage, water and sky. Registration
reconstructs current points from exported depth, uses actual drawn views and camera positions to map
them into the preceding captured output, and bilinearly samples that output. Previous depth rejects
occlusion mismatches above 2.5 percent. Jitter is accounted for in depth lookups. This compensates
translation, rotation and bob as far as low-resolution depth permits; it is not a raw frame difference.

| State | Before registered luma MAE | After | Before/after Laplacian L1 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Standing | 0.726 | 0.581 | 11.118 / 11.159 |
| Walking | 5.604 | 3.346 | 9.926 / 9.810 |

Walking residual falls **40.3 percent**, with only a 1.2 percent sharpness decrease. Standing detail
is unchanged within 0.4 percent; its small 0.145 luma residual difference indicates the noise and
jitter variation between these single short runs. The much larger 2.258 luma walking reduction and
the terrain crops support that missing bob explained **part** of the moving hill instability. It
does not eliminate shimmer: 3.346 remains well above the standing residual. This is one short pair,
not a repeated statistically controlled benchmark or proof about all distant terrain.

`followup/hill-summary.json` and per-frame metadata contain the numbers and poses. The 3x crops and
