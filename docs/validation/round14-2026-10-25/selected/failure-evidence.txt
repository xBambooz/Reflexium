Boundary: a real device loss raised by RenderPearl's VulkanUtils.crashIfFailure (native present or
submit) throws GpuDeviceLossException, and Minecraft has no recovery path for it. The integration
detects the lost result before touching the SDK again, disables DLSS/DLSS-G with the message above,
and stops calling Streamline. Engine-level recovery from a genuinely lost VkDevice is outside the mod.
