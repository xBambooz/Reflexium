Interpretation:
  - DLAA runs at 1:1 and is not sharper than Quality on the same leaves. The residual is therefore
    not the 0.667 upscale ratio alone; it is the temporal reconstruction of alpha-tested cutout leaves.
  - AUTO and Transformer K are identical on crop A. On crop B, K is 2.0 dB worse, so the shipped AUTO
    default is retained.
  - 18 phases (NVIDIA's table minimum for Quality) is worse by 0.3 to 2.7 dB. 64 phases is 0.08 dB
    better on crop A and 2.5 dB worse on crop B. The shipped 32-phase policy is the best compromise.
  - More negative texture bias (-2.085, -2.585) raises Laplacian energy by up to 0.4 but does not
    raise matched detail correlation. That is added edge energy, not recovered detail. The guide bias
    (-1.585) is retained.
  - DLAA samples at 1:1 with bias 0. This is a deliberate deviation from the guide formula's
    unconditional -1 term, because at 1:1 the input is already at display sampling; changing it was
    not shown to recover matched detail in these captures.
  - Exposure needs no input here: the color is finished SDR, pre-exposure is 1.0, and auto exposure is
    off. The pack HDR path was not active.

Conclusion: every numeric prerequisite checked (jitter length/range/sign, mip bias application,
exposure, preset, zero still-frame motion vectors) conforms. The static foliage gap remains a
reconstruction limitation for alpha-cutout leaves, not an input defect. Objective 5 is not fully met.
