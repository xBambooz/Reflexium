## Problems and observations

No crashes. The client stayed alive for the whole run and exited cleanly.

1. `reflexium` takes `key` and `value` params, not direct field names. `{"upscaler": "off"}` returned `ok: true` and changed nothing, with no error. The working form is `{"key": "upscaler", "value": "off"}`. A call with no params returns status only.
2. Turning upscaling off also sets `frame_generation` to `off`. Turning upscaling back on does not restore FG, so FG has to be re-enabled by hand.
3. Auto FG multiplier generated nothing in the window. With `fg_multiplier=auto` and `frame_generation=dlss`, status read `rendered only (frame target 415 FPS, Auto output budget)`, `fg_effective_multiplier` was 1, and `fg_generated_presents` stayed at 4892 for about 20 s. `fg_batches_started` also stayed at 2446. This may be the designed Auto budget behaviour at about 350 FPS against a 415 FPS target, or a bug. This run does not settle which.
4. Read values after a wait. Immediately after `quality=quality`, the response showed `Quality` with the old 853x480 render size. After 5 s it settled to 1707x960.
5. Step 5 ran with shaders off, because the steps run in order and shaders were turned off in step f. The DLSS performance state was otherwise unchanged.
6. Screenshot paths are resolved from the game directory when relative. The screenshot for step b landed under `run\docs\validation`, and a copy was placed in `docs\validation` for this commit.
