# Out-of-box defaults, 19 October 2026

Base: `main` `1ccee81`. Branch: `t3/dlss-defaults-visual-tuning`. No merge to main.

## Decision

Fresh configuration keeps `upscaler=auto` and `upscale_quality=quality`, changes the model to CNN E, requests DLSS frame generation with multiplier Auto (`0`), and keeps Reflex On and Smart Reflex Off. Auto now resolves to native rendering without a shader pack, and DLSS when a shader pack and DLSS runtime are available, otherwise FSR or native. Explicit DLSS/FSR selections still work without shaders. Existing saved choices are preserved, including quality and FG Off. Ultra Performance is never selected by initialization or reset.

CNN E Quality is the cost-conscious starting point. Transformer remains selectable: K for DLAA, Quality, Balanced and now Performance; L for explicitly selected Ultra Performance. Model Auto continues to pass preset zero to NGX. Presets are requests, not independently observed runtime network selections. Sodium reset and vanilla codec fallbacks now use the same model/FG defaults as the config.

Frame-generation Auto refers to its multiplier policy, not an automatic AMD/NVIDIA backend selector. Requested `frame_generation=dlss` activates only through the existing support, runtime, Vulkan, VSync and Reflex gates; unsupported hardware does not execute DLSS FG. AMD default activation was not hardware-tested.

This ships a better quality/cost default, not proof of the universal goal that DLSS is practically lossless and faster than native. The BSL pan still has visible foliage streaking. Shaderless SR is slower than native in these diagnostics, and shader packs can also be geometry-bound. DLAA would add reconstruction cost without reducing scene pixels, so it is not the shaderless Auto choice.

## Captures and inspection

RTX 5070, driver 617.42, Minecraft 26.3 Vulkan, supported experimental Iris and Sodium 0.9.2, BSL v10.1.8. Owned Minecraft MCP client on port 25671, debug port 25672, primary monitor at 2560x1440, isolated copy of the main repository's run directory. The black overlay was left alone. No focus input or other-client shutdown was used.

Save `Reflexium Vitrail Smoke`, spectator at -24.5,131,11.5, yaw/pitch 0/0. Frozen ticks, noon, clear weather. Every arm sets FG Off, Reflex On, sharpness 20 and shadow caps 0/0. Captures ran the copied config's Smart Reflex On and automatic interval setting; the reproduction driver sets these explicitly. Fresh-config validation separately verifies Smart Reflex Off. Model/preset overrides are explicit and identical between static and pan. No mip-bias, jitter or pack-option reduction was used to favor an arm.

Each pack has native, CNN E Quality, Transformer K Quality, K/M Balanced and K/M Performance. Each arm captures 12 static frames and 12 pan frames with `capture_sequence` (100 ms interval, 12-degree yaw over 24 ticks), then a separate four-second profiler sample. All compared camera metadata match exactly at each scored frame. Runtime captures use the unmodified base's rendering implementation; the new changes alter selection defaults only.

All four final contact sheets and representative full frames were opened and inspected. Frames 0 and 11 are shown at original crop scale in the linked sheets:

- [Shaderless static](defaults-evidence-2026-10-19/vanilla-static.png), [shaderless pan](defaults-evidence-2026-10-19/vanilla-pan.png).
- [BSL static](defaults-evidence-2026-10-19/bsl-static.png), [BSL pan](defaults-evidence-2026-10-19/bsl-pan.png).

K preserves more ground/grass texture than M, especially shaderless. M's smoother result does not provide a convincing visual benefit worth its cost here. BSL Performance M has slightly higher ground high-pass correlation than K, but lower PSNR; neither repairs the visible temporal foliage streaks. Balanced K wins both ground metrics in both packs. CNN Quality is close to native in the static image and preserves block texture during the pan, but it is visibly softer than K in BSL. This is a deliberate GPU-cost tradeoff, not an assertion of identical images.

## Metrics and costs

`tools/defaults-validation.py analyze` follows the Round 3 RGB MSE/PSNR approach and adds Laplacian high-pass correlation. Regions include ground, plants and central leaves. [Raw metrics](defaults-evidence-2026-10-19/metrics.json), [all profiles](defaults-evidence-2026-10-19/profiles.json), and [full ground summary](defaults-evidence-2026-10-19/summary.md) are committed. Full sequences, metadata and statuses remain under `build/validation/defaults` in this worktree. Analysis requires local Pillow and NumPy; neither ships with the mod.

Animated foliage, smoke, sky, dither and lighting are not independently phase-locked between arms; their MSE is descriptive only. The lower BSL CNN score is consistent with its softer inspected output. No speed/quality conclusion comes from Laplacian energy alone.

Another harness JVM (PID 15300) rendered alongside owned PID 25440. These are indicative timestamps, not isolated library costs or throughput acceptance. Contention changed over the matrix, evident in K being cheaper later in BSL than earlier shaderless. Do not compare absolute pack costs as if GPU scheduling were constant. M still costs more than K in each local pair. In BSL Performance the difference is 0.262 ms, consistent with the earlier report's roughly 0.3 ms difference.

World scope: shaderless native 0.214 ms versus CNN Quality 0.877 ms; BSL native 4.776 ms versus CNN Quality 4.274 ms and Transformer Quality 4.955 ms. These motivate the selection policy but do not establish an FPS gain. The earlier [performance report](perf-overnight-2026-10-19.md) supplies the less-contended frozen-scene evidence: BSL native 301.4 FPS, CNN Quality 310.3, Transformer Quality 271.6 with shadow caps off. No shadow quality is lowered by these defaults.

## Verification and remaining

[Title status](defaults-evidence-2026-10-19/fresh-title.json), [BSL status](defaults-evidence-2026-10-19/fresh-shaders-1.json), [shaderless status](defaults-evidence-2026-10-19/fresh-shaders-0.json). FG Auto selected supported dynamic multipliers and generated-present counters advanced; this is functional compatibility evidence, not cadence/scanout acceptance. The launch reported `focus_unchanged=true`.

Owned shutdown uses the Minecraft MCP dispatcher's authenticated PID/directory/jar ownership verification and `quit {exit:1}`. Both successful clients are shut down this way. The fresh-config test is runnable with `tools/defaults-validation.py fresh`; it fails if any expected initialization default is wrong. Captures and analysis are `capture` and `analyze`. The small `defaults-mcp.mjs` adapter calls the existing Minecraft MCP dispatcher because this host did not expose its tools directly.

Remaining: independent material-motion quality, water/refraction/particles/volumetric layer reconstruction, isolated representative throughput acceptance, physical scanout/cadence, and AMD/unsupported-runtime hardware fallback acceptance. The existing IQ report describes these reconstruction limits. A preset/default change does not solve them.

Reference implementation reviewed: [Super Resolution config](https://github.com/IReallyWantToSleep/superresolution/blob/dev/common/src/main/java/io/homo/superresolution/common/config/SuperResolutionConfig.java), linked from its [Modrinth project](https://modrinth.com/mod/superresolution). Its runtime algorithm selection is distinct from persisted settings; Reflexium reuses its existing resolver and support gates rather than adding a dependency or a second configuration system.
