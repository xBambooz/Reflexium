## Technical limits and conclusions

1. **Submitted rate vs physical scanout**: Reflexium measures and reports operations submitted to the Vulkan presentation queue. It does not claim physical monitor scanout or panel flip synchronization.
2. **Reflex driver pacing**: The high-FPS pacing overhead and timing inversions under driver 617.42 reproduce in isolated C++ references without Minecraft or Java. Reflexium handles driver return codes and timeouts without hanging, but cannot fix underlying driver-level queue pacing quirks.
3. **Hardware coverage**: Earlier DLSS/MFG and forced AMD-scheduler functional tests ran on RTX 5070 Vulkan. They predate the changed synchronization/lifetime behavior. Native AMD/Intel acceptance remains open.
