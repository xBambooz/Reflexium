Observation
- The relative Laplacian loss is uniform across cutout leaves, stone, wood and grass (about 35 percent at
  Quality K; about 60 to 65 percent of native Laplacian retained). Foliage is not an outlier.
- There is no whole-frame or subpixel misalignment: an integer shift search found 0,0 best, and a bilinear
  search at 0.1 px steps from -1.0 to +1.0 px also found 0,0 on leafA and solid.
- Consecutive DLSS DLAA frames are stable (crop PSNR 46.6 and 46.2 between three captures), so the loss is
  a converged reconstruction difference, not temporal noise.
- Disabling jitter produces the near-native static image at 1:1, but destroys the 0.667-ratio Quality image.
  The jitter is doing the work the model expects; the static softness is the model's temporal filter on
  hard-edged pixel-art textures, not an input-level defect isolated to alpha cutout.
