# Round 15 validation: static foliage investigation and fullscreen handling

Run 2026-10-25 on `round6-validation` (PR 1) on top of `8419d45`, dev client only, RTX 5070,
NVIDIA 617.42, Minecraft 26.3 Vulkan, Streamline 2.14.16. Harness branch `fullscreen-opt-in`
commit `0b90ab5` was used for the fullscreen tests; the foliage tests used the same harness without
the fullscreen opt-in.

The final-environment restriction stays in force: Ryan may use the Modrinth profile, so the profile
was not launched, installed into or touched. The dev client ran on the secondary XF250Q, never
focused except for one fullscreen batch (each focused test under 30 s), and the previous foreground
window was restored and verified afterwards. The OLED overlay was not touched.

## Changes in this round

1. **Harness fullscreen opt-in.** `mc-agent-harness` now accepts `-Fullscreen`
   (`agentharness.fullscreen` / `AGENTHARNESS_FULLSCREEN`) and a `fullscreen` endpoint
   (`fullscreen=1|0`, optional `activate=1`). Without the flag the existing `Window.setFullscreen`
   cancellation is unchanged. With the flag, a fullscreen request is allowed only on the launch
   monitor: a window moved to another display is returned to the target display before the request.
   Status reports requested, SDL, borderless, exclusive and display fields under `window.fullscreen`.
   The change is committed on harness branch `fullscreen-opt-in`, not `main`.
2. **Renderer code:** none. The measured inputs did not support an input-level correction, so no
   foliage change was shipped. The decision-log line for objective 1 was committed with the round.

## 1. Objective 5 static foliage

### Method

Two static poses in `Reflexium Vitrail Smoke`, vanilla renderer, 1920x1080, render distance 12,
frozen ticks, noon, clear weather:

- Range: `200.5 / 151 / 0.5`, yaw 60, pitch 3 (round 14 pose).
- Forest: `200.5 / 67 / 150`, yaw 90, pitch 0 (the documented forest pose).

Every arm waited at least 2.5 s after the mode switch and took two captures 0.8 s apart; repeats
agreed. Native repeats were identical on every crop. Metrics are PSNR (RGB 0 to 255), high-pass
detail correlation and mean absolute Laplacian on fixed matched crops. Range crops: leafA and leafB
(the leaf tower), solid (stone pavement, no foliage) and frame (stone and wood). Forest crops: leafF
(tree canopy), wall, grass and trunk.

### Cutout-specific audit

- **Discard LOD.** Sodium 0.9.2 `block_layer_opaque.fsh` samples `u_BlockTex` once, multiplies the
  vertex color, and applies `if (color.a < ALPHA_CUTOUT) discard;` to that same sample. There is no
  separate unbiased alpha sample. The run used `pixel_filtering_mode: NEAREST` (the
  `sampleNearest`/`textureGrad` path), where Vulkan applies the sampler `mipLodBias`; telemetry
  recorded 3 of 3 `u_BlockTex` binds with `applied_bias=-1.5849625`. The discard therefore uses the
  biased LOD consistently. The alternative RGSS path calls `textureLod`, which would ignore the
  sampler bias; it was not active at this setting.
- **Mip-level alpha erosion.** Vanilla 26.3 generates cutout mips with its coverage fitter, and
  `oak_leaves.png.mcmeta` selects `dark_cutout` (round 7 source audit). It is not a box filter. The
  bias sweep confirms the bias is not the missing lever: removing it (bias -1.0 or 0) does not
  improve leaf PSNR or correlation on either pose and slightly worsens the forest wall at bias 0.
- **Alpha-to-coverage.** This renderer has no MSAA or alpha-to-coverage path; cutout terrain is
  discard at one sample. A2C is not available without an engine-level sample-count change and was
  not simulated.
- **Reactive and transparency mask.** Streamline's DLSS options have no reactive-mask input, and the
  transformer presets K/L/M do not take the legacy current-color-bias mask. `alphaUpscalingEnabled`
  only upscales the alpha channel of the color texture; surviving cutout fragments are opaque in
  alpha, so it cannot change cutout RGB. These were checked in the SDK headers, not invoked.
- **Preset and model.** Auto/K is the best overall. Preset E is 0.05 to 0.5 dB better on several leaf and
  stone crops and up to 0.4 dB worse on others; it did not produce a consistent win. J matches K, L
  and M are clearly worse. E is also a deprecated legacy preset, so the shipped Auto/K default is
  retained.
- **Alignment.** An integer shift search found 0,0 best. A bilinear search at 0.1 px steps from -1.0
  to +1.0 px also found 0,0 on leafA and solid, so the gap is not a misalignment or a bilinear
  resample.
- **Temporal stability.** Three consecutive DLAA captures at the same pose differ by only 46.6 and
  46.2 dB on the leaf crop, so the measured gap is a converged reconstruction difference, not
  frame-to-frame noise.

### Finding

The softness is **not specific to alpha-cutout foliage**. On the same frame, the relative Laplacian
loss is uniform across leaves, stone pavement, stone and wood framing, grass and trunk: Quality
retains about 57 to 66 percent of native Laplacian on every crop. Round 14 measured only leaf crops
and attributed the gap to alpha-test reconstruction. The extra opaque controls show that the model
softens hard-edged pixel-art textures generally at this pose.

The discriminating experiment is jitter. With projection jitter disabled, DLAA at 1:1 returns to
39.5 dB and 0.982 correlation with 93 percent of native Laplacian on the forest canopy, and 32.1 dB
with 86 percent on the range leaf crop. But the same setting at Quality (0.667 ratio) collapses to
20.2 dB and 0.408 correlation because the upscaler needs jitter to reconstruct detail. Disabling
jitter is therefore not a shippable correction: it removes temporal anti-aliasing and reintroduces
the aliasing and shimmer objective 5 forbids. No sharpening was added.

The honest residual is the DLSS model's temporal reconstruction of hard-edged pixel-art content
with the required jitter, not jitter length/sign, mip bias, mip alpha coverage, LOD consistency, a
missing cutout mask or preset choice. Objective 5 static detail remains **not fully met**. The
shipped defaults are unchanged.

## Verdict

The harness now has a sanctioned fullscreen path confined to the target monitor, and the
windowed/fullscreen/alt-tab/resize matrix passes with DLSS SR, 3x DLSS-G and Reflex active. The
static foliage investigation found no input-level defect: the discard shares the biased atlas LOD,
the atlas mip chain preserves cutout coverage, bias and preset changes do not recover detail, and
the loss is uniform on opaque pixel-art surfaces, not cutout-specific. No fix was shipped and
objective 5 static detail is not claimed as met.
