## Comparisons and their limits

Fresh shaderless native, DLAA, Quality and Performance stills and turn/strafe/hand sequences are under `build/validation/recovery/shaders-off`. Shader-on native, DLAA, Quality and Performance captures are under the matching `build/validation/recovery/shaders-on` tree (177 files, `index.json`); the TAA-bypass comparison is in `pack-taa`. Both trees are ignored build output, not committed. The earlier worker baseline and after1 trees are preserved, not relabeled as new measurements.

The initial cutout comparison was invalid: the arena placed nonpersistent leaves without supporting logs, so they decayed between modes. The screenshots show different geometry and dropped saplings. The capture script now places persistent leaves. Those comparisons must be recaptured; do not interpret their low image correlation as reconstruction failure.

For the valid shaderless wall crop, high-pass correlation to that run's native image is 0.8703 DLAA, 0.8644 Quality and 0.8211 Performance. The corresponding MAE is 1.425, 1.450 and 1.829 on 8-bit luminance. These values compare current modes to native, not improvement against an unchanged historical baseline. Native pixel boundaries remain sharper in the inspected enlarged wall crop. Higher Laplacian energy alone would reward noise and is not used as an acceptance criterion.

The TAA-off diagnostic improved the recovery shader-wall crop's detail correlation from about 0.69 to 0.87, but changed effect sampling as well. That broad toggle is not the default. The narrower adapter preserves the sampling branches and removes only the final resolve. The inspected enlarged hay/iron crop retains visible texel boundaries that the retained resolve softens. Its eight-frame stationary wall sequence has mean inter-frame luma change 0.5566 versus 0.4948 with the old retained resolve (8-bit values). This small increase does not establish noise-free output across shadowed scenes or all pack settings.

Fresh default-policy evidence in `build/validation/final-default` confirms shader-enabled Quality at 1280x720 to 1920x1080, DLSS/FSR switching, restored native shader behavior when off, and bypass reapplication when re-enabled. Wall correlation against that run's native reference is 0.8613 DLSS and 0.8601 FSR; MAE is 2.147 and 2.129. These current-mode comparisons are not a historical before/after benchmark. A six-frame smooth turn leaves only the initial rebuild reset and no error. `resolve-adapter2/mode-checks.json`, `model-*.json`, `camera-mode.json`, `rejoin.json`, `native-restored.json` and `re-enabled.json` retain the earlier live transition evidence. The FG smoke status recorded successful evaluation and generated presents; it does not establish cadence.

Final logs contain stale Vulkan-layer manifest registrations from the separate Reflex investigation checkout and dev Realms authentication failures. They contain no new Reflexium evaluation exception or Vulkan validation VUID. Core/synchronization validation was not enabled for this upscaling pass. The owned final harness client (port 25637) was shut down and options.txt, vitrail/pack.txt and reflexium.properties restored from the saved backups.

The supported pack fallback still has two full-scene temporal filters and late LDR input. The source architecture review does not establish how much of the user's blur each stage causes. No claim of matching native in all motion, matching hypothetical Mojang internals, vendor certification, AMD/Intel hardware acceptance or physical display cadence is made.
